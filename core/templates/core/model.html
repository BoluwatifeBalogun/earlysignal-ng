{% icon "info" %}The bundled dataset is synthetic. It was generated to follow Nigerian assessment rules, not collected from any institution. Retrain on real, anonymised records before relying on these figures.

Method

  1. Duplicates are removed. Records missing any indicator are excluded, never estimated.
  2. Names are dropped. Only CA score, attendance and the previous result (as a percentage of its GPA scale) reach the model.
  3. The data is split 80/20, keeping the same share of under-performing students in both parts.
  4. Both models weight the under-performing minority more heavily, so they are not rewarded for ignoring it.
  5. 5-fold cross-validation on the training part checks that the scores hold across different splits.
  6. The model with the better F1-score is deployed. On a near tie the simpler Logistic Regression is kept.
  7. The deployed model's score is calibrated, so the chance shown to a lecturer matches how often students with that score really under-performed.
