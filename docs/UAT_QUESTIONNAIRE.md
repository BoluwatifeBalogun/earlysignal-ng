# User acceptance testing: EarlySignal NG

For section 3.10.3 of the write-up. Give this to a small sample of lecturers and academic
advisers after they have used the system. It follows the Technology Acceptance Model
(section 2.3): perceived ease of use, perceived usefulness, and intention to use.

## Tasks to complete first

Sign in with the account provided, then:

1. Add a new student and enter one academic record.
2. Request a prediction for that record.
3. Read the result page and say, in your own words, why the student got that risk band.
4. Import the sample class list (CSV) and find the students at high risk.
5. Open the prediction history and filter it to one geopolitical zone.

Observer notes for each task: completed without help (yes or no), time taken, errors made.
