From a result sheet to a conversation, in four steps.

Most intervention starts after a poor result is already on the transcript. This moves it to the middle of the semester.

  1. Record

    Enter a student's CA score, attendance and previous GPA, or import a whole class from a spreadsheet.

  2. Predict

    The trained model estimates the chance that the student under-performs this semester and places them in a low, medium or high risk band.

  3. Explain

    Every prediction shows how much each of the three figures pushed the estimate up or down, in plain words.

  4. Act

    The lecturer decides what happens next. Each prediction stays in the history so progress can be reviewed after the next CA.
